TACTICAL DIGITAL DEFENSE TIPS

Actionable rules every citizen must follow to prevent account compromise.

RULE #01
Passphrase Shield

Combine 4 unrelated words into long passphrases (e.g., correct-horse-battery-staple-9#). Length beats complexity against GPU dictionary attack tools!

RULE #02
Root Domain Check

Scammers use fake subdomains like onlinesbi.update-login.xyz. Always verify the characters right before the first single slash!

RULE #03
App-Based 2FA

Prefer Authenticator apps (Google/Microsoft Authenticator) over SMS OTPs. SMS OTPs are vulnerable to SIM swap and SS7 interception attacks!

RULE #04
Zero-Trust Wi-Fi

Never access banking apps or UPI accounts on free public Wi-Fi networks without an encrypted VPN tunnel. Beware of Evil Twin spoofed hotspots!
