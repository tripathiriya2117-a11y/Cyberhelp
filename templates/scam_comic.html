EASY MONEY = DANGER
PANEL 1 SETUP: THE BAIT
SCENE: WHATSAPP MESSAGE
UNKNOWNSENDER
Hey! Earn ₹150-₹500 daily by liking YouTube videos & writing reviews.
No experience needed. Instant payout!
Want to join the group?
UNSOLICITED "EASY MONEY" MESSAGE
VICTIM (Rahul): "Extra income sounds perfect. Let me check this out..."
Scammers reach out with attractive offers on WhatsApp - liking videos, hotel reviews, or simple tasks for quick cash.
PANEL 2 TRICK: TRUST BUILDING
SCENE: TELEGRAM VIP GROUP
VIP PREPAID TASKS
Complete 3 tasks → Get ₹450 credited
VIP LEVEL 5: DEPOSIT ₹50,000 TO UNLOCK EARNINGS
Once deposited, you can withdraw ALL + commissions
THE PUSH TO DEPOSIT
SCAMMER: "You've earned trust! To unlock your full balance, you must deposit a small amount into our merchant portal."
After small payouts build confidence, victims are moved to private Telegram groups and pressured to deposit larger amounts into fake portals.
PANEL 3 REALIZATION: RED FLAGS
SCENE: DOUBT KICKS IN
SPOTTED RED FLAGS
  • Easy money: Unrealistic daily payouts for simple clicks.
  • Off-platform move: Shifted to private Telegram/VIP group.
  • Pay to earn: Asked to deposit money to "unlock" earnings.
  • Escalation: Demands grow from ₹50k to lakhs.
VICTIM: "Wait... why do I need to PAY to get MY money? This doesn't feel right!"
THE CRUCIAL MOMENT - TRUST YOUR GUT!
PANEL 4 RESPONSE: STOP & ACT
SCENE: SMART CHOICES
STOP. BLOCK. REPORT.
VICTIM: "I'm not sending any more money. I'm blocking and reporting this right now!"
Never pay to earn. Exit the group, block the numbers, preserve screenshots, and report immediately.
SAFETY PLAYBOOK

FINAL PRACTICAL SAFETY GUIDE & REPORTING

GOLDEN HOUR MATTERS
DO NOT
  • Pay registration/deposit fees to get a job
  • Move conversations to private Telegram groups
  • Share UPI PINs, OTPs, or banking details
  • Install unknown remote access apps
DO
  • Verify jobs only via official company portals
  • Screenshot messages, numbers, UPI IDs, links
  • Block and report suspicious contacts
  • Talk to trusted family/friends before paying
REPORT IMMEDIATELY

Call 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline) within 1-2 hours for financial fraud.

File at cybercrime.gov.in

REMEMBER

Golden Rule: Legitimate employers never ask you to pay to get paid.
